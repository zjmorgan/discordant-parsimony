bin_factor(d, widths, projection=None)[source]#

Attenuation of pair terms by averaging over histogram bins.

A bin spans widths \(\Delta u_a\) along the axes of the volume, whose Miller indices are \(\mathbf{h} = W\mathbf{u}\). Since \(\mathbf{h}\cdot\mathbf{d} = \mathbf{u}\cdot(W^\mathsf{T} \mathbf{d})\), averaging \(e^{2\pi i \mathbf{h}\cdot\mathbf{d}}\) over the bin multiplies a term by

\[\prod_a \operatorname{sinc}\!\left(\Delta u_a\, (W^\mathsf{T}\mathbf{d})_a\right).\]
Parameters:
dndarray, (n, 3)

Pair vectors in fractional coordinates.

widthsarray_like, (3,)

Bin widths along the volume axes u.

projectionndarray, (3, 3), optional

The projection matrix \(W\) of the volume; identity (axes along h, k, l) by default.

Returns:
factorndarray, (n,)
design_matrix(h, orbits, sites, structure, widths=None, thermal=True, b=None, max_elements=4194304, operators=None, projection=None)[source]#

Intensity per unit covariance of each pair orbit.

Column \(k\) is

\[A_k(\mathbf{h}) = \frac{1}{n_W} \sum_{W} \sum_{p \in k} b_i b_j\, e^{-W_i - W_j} \cos(2\pi\, \mathbf{h} \cdot R_W\mathbf{d}_p)\, \beta(R_W \mathbf{d}_p),\]

averaged over rotations \(R_W\) (h @ W), with the bin factor \(\beta\) of bin_factor() and Debye-Waller factors at \(\mathbf{h} R_W\), so that \(I = A \mathbf{c}\) for orbit coefficients \(\mathbf{c}\). The bin is the measured one around \(\mathbf{h}\) for every image, which is why the rotation acts on the pair vector inside the bin factor.

displacement_design_matrix(h, orbits, tensors, sites, structure, widths=None, thermal=True, b=None, max_elements=4194304, operators=None, projection=None)[source]#

Intensity per unit displacement-displacement coefficient.

To second order in the displacement \(\mathbf{v} = n\mathbf{u}\) of present atoms, correlated displacements contribute

\[I_\mathrm{disp}(\mathbf{Q}) = \sum_p b_i b_j\, (\mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{T}} V_p \mathbf{Q}) \cos(2\pi\, \mathbf{h} \cdot \mathbf{d}_p)\, e^{-W_i - W_j}, \qquad V_p = \langle \mathbf{v}_i(\mathbf{0})\, \mathbf{v}_j(\mathbf{R})^{\mathsf{T}} \rangle,\]

with \(V_p = \sum_k \theta_k V_{pk}\) from tensor_orbits(). It grows as \(|\mathbf{Q}|^2\). Debye-Waller, bin and symmetry averaging follow design_matrix().

evaluate(h, terms, structure, max_elements=4194304, check=False)[source]#

Diffuse intensity of pair terms at arbitrary reciprocal positions.

Computes

\[I(\mathbf{Q}) = \sum_p w_p \cos(2\pi\, \mathbf{h} \cdot \mathbf{d}_p) \exp\left(-\tfrac{1}{2} \mathbf{Q}^\mathsf{T} U_p \mathbf{Q}\right)\]

by direct summation in tiles of at most max_elements Q-term products, never forming the full Q-by-terms matrix.

size_effect_design_matrix(h, orbits, vectors, sites, structure, widths=None, thermal=True, b=None, max_elements=4194304, operators=None, projection=None)[source]#

Intensity per unit size-effect coefficient.

To first order in the displacement \(\mathbf{v} = n\mathbf{u}\) of present atoms, the occupation-displacement cross term is

\[I_\mathrm{size}(\mathbf{Q}) = -\sum_p b_i b_j\, (\mathbf{Q} \cdot \boldsymbol{\sigma}_p) \sin(2\pi\, \mathbf{h} \cdot \mathbf{d}_p), \qquad \boldsymbol{\sigma}_p = \langle \delta n_i(\mathbf{0})\, \mathbf{v}_j(\mathbf{R}) \rangle - \langle \mathbf{v}_i(\mathbf{0})\, \delta n_j(\mathbf{R}) \rangle,\]

with \(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_p = \sum_k \theta_k \mathbf{v}_{pk}\) from vector_orbits(). It is odd in \(\mathbf{Q} - \mathbf{G}\) about Bragg positions (asymmetric diffuse) and grows linearly with \(|\mathbf{Q}|\). Debye-Waller, bin and symmetry averaging follow design_matrix().
