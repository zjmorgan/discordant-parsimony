discordant_parsimony package#

discordant_parsimony models single-crystal diffuse scattering from finite-range pair correlations and refines those correlations against measured intensities.

Features#

  • Average structure from CIF with symmetry expansion of anisotropic displacement parameters

  • Split-site clusters and forbidden contacts from interatomic distances

  • Pair orbits of the space group within a distance cutoff

  • Design matrix of the intensity per unit correlation, with exact histogram-bin averaging

  • Constrained linear refinement and participation scans

  • Real-space 3D-\(\Delta\)PDF of any set of pair terms

Workflow modules#

discordant_parsimony.structure module#

Average crystal structure: lattice, symmetry, and atomic sites.

Conventions#

Fractional coordinates \(\mathbf{x}\) and Miller indices \(\mathbf{h}\) (r.l.u.) refer to the cell given in the CIF. Direct basis vectors are the columns of \(A\) (Cartesian, \(\mathring{\mathrm{A}}\)), with \(\mathbf{a}\) along Cartesian \(x\) and \(\mathbf{b}\) in the \(xy\) plane. Reciprocal basis vectors (without the \(2\pi\)) are the columns of \(B = (A^{-1})^\mathsf{T}\), so that

\[\mathbf{Q} = 2\pi B \mathbf{h}, \qquad \mathbf{Q} \cdot (A \mathbf{x}) = 2\pi\, \mathbf{h} \cdot \mathbf{x},\]

with \(\mathbf{Q}\) in \(\mathring{\mathrm{A}}^{-1}\). Displacement parameters \(U\) are stored as Cartesian covariance tensors in \(\mathring{\mathrm{A}}^2\).

discordant_parsimony.rules module#

Local occupation rules derived from the average structure geometry.

Two kinds of rule are identified from interatomic contacts among the participating positions:

  • split-site clusters: positions closer than cluster_distance are alternatives, at most one of which is occupied (an atom and its split partners, or a site and its substituent);

  • forbidden contacts: pairs of positions in different clusters closer than a physically allowed distance, which cannot both be occupied.

discordant_parsimony.symmetry module#

Symmetry-independent pair correlations.

A pair \((i, j, \mathbf{R})\) joins position \(i\) in the origin cell to position \(j\) in cell \(\mathbf{R}\). A space-group operator \((W, \mathbf{t})\) maps it to \((i', j', \mathbf{R}')\) with

\[W \mathbf{x}_i + \mathbf{t} = \mathbf{x}_{i'} + \mathbf{L}_i, \quad W \mathbf{x}_j + \mathbf{t} = \mathbf{x}_{j'} + \mathbf{L}_j, \quad \mathbf{R}' = W \mathbf{R} + \mathbf{L}_j - \mathbf{L}_i;\]

pair reversal maps it to \((j, i, -\mathbf{R})\). Correlations with the full symmetry of the average structure are equal on each orbit, so one coefficient per orbit describes them. Pairs are selected with a distance cutoff, which is invariant under the group.

discordant_parsimony.correlations module#

Occupation covariances.

\[C_{ij}(\mathbf{R}) = \langle \delta n_i(\mathbf{0})\, \delta n_j(\mathbf{R}) \rangle, \qquad \delta n = n - \langle n \rangle.\]

Covariances may come from an analytic model or be estimated from occupation configurations of a periodic lattice of cells. Only the participating positions are represented; this is not an atomistic supercell of the full structure.

discordant_parsimony.constraints module#

Exact linear constraints on orbit coefficients and their solution.

Two kinds of relation are imposed, both independent of any model of the local order:

  • sum rules: a split-site cluster without vacancies always holds exactly one atom, so the fluctuations of its members sum to zero and their covariances with any position sum to zero, \(\sum_a C(a \,@\, \mathbf{o}_a,\, j \,@\, \mathbf{R}) = 0\);

  • homometric ties: orbits whose pair vectors, scattering-length products and displacement tensors coincide give identical intensities, so only their sum is measurable; they are tied equal (each then carries the average of the group).

Together with coefficients fixed by the average structure they define \(\mathbf{c} = \mathbf{c}_0 + N \boldsymbol{\theta}\) with free parameters \(\boldsymbol{\theta}\).

discordant_parsimony.pairs module#

Weighted Gaussian pair terms: the common currency of all models.

Every nuclear diffuse model is reduced to terms \((\mathbf{d}_p, w_p, U_p)\) with

\[I(\mathbf{Q}) = \sum_p w_p \, e^{i \mathbf{Q} \cdot \mathbf{d}_p} \exp\left(-\tfrac{1}{2} \mathbf{Q}^\mathsf{T} U_p \mathbf{Q}\right),\]

equivalently a 3D-\(\Delta\)PDF made of Gaussians of weight \(w_p\) and covariance \(U_p\) centered on pair vectors \(\mathbf{d}_p\). Occupational disorder on split sites gives \(w = b_i b_j C_{ij}(\mathbf{R})\) at \(\mathbf{d} = \mathbf{R} + \mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i\) with \(U = U_i + U_j\) for uncorrelated thermal motion. Term sets must be closed under \((\mathbf{d}, w, U) \to (-\mathbf{d}, w, U)\), which holds for any covariance with \(C_{ij}(\mathbf{R}) = C_{ji}(-\mathbf{R})\); the intensity is then real.

discordant_parsimony.selection module#

Crystallographic selection of reciprocal-space positions.

Measured volumes are reduced to the positions that carry independent diffuse information:

  • Bragg regions are excluded around reflections allowed by the lattice centering;

  • symmetrized data are reduced to one representative per Laue orbit;

  • diffuse features are targeted like satellites, at points \(\mathbf{G} + \mathbf{k}\) or along lines \(\mathbf{G} + \mathbf{k} + t \mathbf{v}\) for allowed \(\mathbf{G}\) and the Laue star of \(\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\);

  • a reproducible random fraction of the remaining positions samples the background between features.

discordant_parsimony.refine module#

Linear least-squares refinement of pair-correlation coefficients.

The model for observed intensities \(y\) with uncertainties \(\sigma\) is

\[y = s\,(A_\mathrm{fixed} \mathbf{c}_\mathrm{fixed} + A_\mathrm{free} \mathbf{c}_\mathrm{free}) + B \boldsymbol{\beta},\]

where \(A\) holds design columns of the pair orbits, \(\mathbf{c}_\mathrm{fixed}\) are coefficients known exactly from the average structure (they set the absolute scale), \(s\) is the scale factor and \(B\) a smooth background basis. With \(\mathbf{u} = s\,\mathbf{c}_\mathrm{free}\) the problem is linear in \((s, \mathbf{u}, \boldsymbol{\beta})\) and is solved in one weighted least-squares step.

discordant_parsimony.participation module#

Which atoms participate in the correlated disorder?

Candidate units (sets of site labels, typically one split-site cluster type each) are switched on and off. A unit that does not participate is uncorrelated with every other position: all its orbit coefficients beyond the exact within-cluster values are zero. Every combination of units is refined on training positions and scored on held-out ones, so models with more parameters gain nothing unless they predict unseen intensities better.

discordant_parsimony.realspace module#

Real-space view of pair terms: the model 3D-\(\Delta\)PDF.

The pair terms \((\mathbf{d}_p, w_p, U_p)\) define

\[\Delta P(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_p w_p\, G(\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{d}_p;\, U_p),\]

whose Fourier transform is the diffuse intensity evaluated by discordant_parsimony.scattering.evaluate().

delta_pdf_slab(terms, structure, x, y, z_range, window=5.0)[source]#

Model 3D-delta-PDF averaged over a slab of constant z.
